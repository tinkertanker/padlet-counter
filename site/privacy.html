Privacy policy

This policy covers the Section Counter for Padlet Chrome extension and the “Count Padlet entries” bookmarklet, both published by Tinkertanker. Last updated 23 September 2026.

Summary

Section Counter for Padlet does not collect, store, or share any personal data. Everything it does happens inside your browser, on the Padlet page you have open.

What it reads

  • The extension only runs on pages at padlet.com and padlet.org (including their subdomains). The bookmarklet only runs when you click it.
  • It reads the page to find section headers and posts, and each post’s colour and pinned state, so that it can display counts.
  • Padlet only shows some posts when they are scrolled into view. To count those too, the counter asks Padlet’s own website for the board’s list of posts, in the same way the Padlet page does. This request goes only to Padlet, uses the Padlet session you are already signed in with, and returns only boards you can already see.

What it does not do

  • It does not send any data to Tinkertanker or to any other third party.
  • It does not use analytics, tracking, advertising, or cookies of its own.
  • It does not save anything: counts are worked out afresh each time the page changes and disappear when you close the page.
  • It does not change your Padlet, its posts, or your account.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this address with a new date.

Section Counter for Padlet is an independent tool and is not affiliated with, or endorsed by, Padlet. Questions can be sent to Tinkertanker via tinkertanker.com.
